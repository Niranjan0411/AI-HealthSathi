# AI HealthSathi 🏥

AI HealthSathi is a modern healthcare assistance web application designed to help users manage and monitor their health through a single, easy-to-use platform.

The application provides features such as an AI-based symptom checker, medicine reminders, health monitoring, emergency SOS, doctor consultation, health tips, and mood tracking.

> ⚠️ Disclaimer: AI HealthSathi is a demonstration/project application and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## ✨ Features

### 🩺 AI Symptom Checker
- Enter and describe symptoms.
- Select common symptoms quickly.
- Provides AI-based health analysis.
- Displays possible conditions and severity.
- Provides general health recommendations.
- Indicates when professional medical help should be considered.

### 💊 Medicine Reminders
- Add medicines with dosage and frequency.
- Set multiple medicine timings.
- Add start and end dates.
- Mark medicines as taken.
- Edit or delete medicine reminders.
- Track medicine history.

### 📊 Health Dashboard
Track important health information including:

- Blood pressure
- Heart rate
- Weight
- Blood sugar
- Daily steps
- Water intake
- Body temperature

The dashboard also provides graphical visualization of health metrics.

### 🚨 Emergency SOS
- Quick-access SOS button.
- Manage emergency contacts.
- Add, edit, and delete emergency contacts.
- Access emergency services.
- Provides emergency service information.

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Connect
Users can explore doctors based on their specialization and availability.

Features include:

- Doctor profiles
- Specializations
- Experience
- Ratings and reviews
- Qualifications
- Languages
- Consultation fees
- Chat consultation
- Video consultation
- Phone consultation

### 💚 Health Tips & Wellness
Provides health and wellness information covering:

- Mental health
- Physical health
- Nutrition
- Sleep
- Emotional wellness
- Preventive care

### 😊 Mood Tracking
Users can record their mood and related activities.

Available mood categories include:

- Excellent
- Good
- Neutral
- Sad
- Stressed

Users can also add notes and activities such as exercise, meditation, yoga, reading, and family time.
